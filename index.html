How to Top-up

Check your remaining data and add more in five steps, straight from our website.

1

Go to our website

Open global-ez.com in your browser, then tap the menu (☰).

2

Log in with your email address

Tap Login, then enter your email and password. You can also continue with Google.

3

Paste your ICCID number on the Check Usage page

Open Check Usage from the menu. Copy the ICCID from your order confirmation email and paste it into the box.

4

See your remaining data

Your remaining data and validity are shown right away.

5

Top up from the website

Open Top Up from the menu, enter your ICCID and tap Check Packages to pick a top-up.
